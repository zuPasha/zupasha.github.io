# zuPasha · TsuPasai · VantaPsy

This is the website for zuPasha, creator of VantaPsy, working under the studio name TsuPasai. It brings the game, the lore, and the commission work into one place, since none of it is really separate. It all grows out of the same world and the same question.

## Three Names, One Project

**zuPasha** is the creator. A stylised contraction of Zuhayr Pasha: Zuhayr meaning bright, Pasha meaning general or leader. This is the name behind the work.

**TsuPasai** is the studio. Built by reshaping how a Japanese speaker might pronounce zuPasha, then compressing it into something new. Roughly: moon soul leader, or guided psyche. To English speakers it sounds like "Super Psy," which is exactly the point.

**VantaPsy** is the world. Vanta, to lack or want. Psy, soul. Together: to lack a soul, or to want one. That question sits behind every story, character, and system built under the name.

## What's Here

- **Home**: zuPasha, and the three disciplines (games, books, art) that all grow from VantaPsy.
- **About**: who's behind the work, and the Six Steps of Humanity that shape every major character arc.
- **Soul Snatcher**: the hub for VantaPsy: Soul Snatcher, the flagship Ren'Py JRPG. Story, characters, screenshots, and where development currently stands.
- **Books**: the VantaPsy Chronology, starting with the Soul Axiom sub-series, set on the fractured world of Aerisu.
- **Portfolio**: character design, illustration, and concept work, browsable by category.
- **Contact**: commission pricing, process, rights and usage terms, and a way to reach out.

## Status

The game is in active development, the Chronology is written and being prepared for release, and commissions are open to help fund both. The site reflects that: built and live, but still filling in around the edges as art, screenshots, and book covers come together. Anything that looks unfinished is a placeholder, not a mistake.
