Overview

A city folded in on itself

Sengetsuki is a coastal town of stacked concrete terraces and ageing infrastructure, locked in eternal night since the airship Nightjar crashed nine years ago. The player takes the role of a Soulless protagonist racing a generous but very real death timer, while investigating the disappearances attributed to a rumoured killer: the Soul Snatcher.

The map plays like a metroidvania, folded and layered rather than sprawling. Investigation, dialogue, combat, and exploration sit in the same space at once. A conversation can unlock a location. A location can change a relationship. Nothing in VantaPsy happens in isolation.

Story

What's actually going on

Within Sengetsuki, the Soulless are treated as incomplete people, written off as a biological certainty and a social sentence. Soul Snatcher follows that assumption all the way down, and asks what a soul actually is: a possession, or something earned through choice, connection, and responsibility.

Dual protagonists, Zuki and Luna, split the early game between two very different ways of facing the same loss. Six main endings wait at the far side of the investigation, depending on how much the player chose to notice along the way.

Characters

Who you'll meet

Full bios and art live in the Compendium in-game. Here's the short version of the people you'll actually travel with.

Canonical Protagonist

Zuki Sapha

Soulless. Curious to a fault, bad at reading rooms, worse at understanding why anyone would cry over a clue. The investigator at the centre of the mystery.

Alternate Protagonist

Luna-Bright Sapha

The older sister who reads every room and carries every responsibility, until combat lets her grin without meaning anything by it.

Fairy Companion

Vixpsy

A Pixpsy who joins on the mountain, early enough to see almost everything that follows. Equal parts light source and lorekeeper, narrating the world while everyone else just tries to survive it.

Party Member

Nathalia "Naxin" Nura

Uses strength as armour until the story asks her to find out what strength actually means.

Party Member

Rakia Sapha

The youngest Sapha sibling, treating grief like a problem that can be solved through ritual, superstition, and exactly the right amount of risk.

Party Member

Xiro Xeraphina

Heir to Xeraphina Auto and Zuki's childhood friend. Performs competence so well that even he sometimes forgets he's performing it.

Party Member

Ezra Zealous

A hacker-rebel leader investigating the Soul Snatcher from the outside, carrying a debt he never agreed to and can't quite set down.

Party Member

Kiara-Koto Najma

Two sisters with a tangled, complicated history, recruited late and still working out what coexisting actually looks like for them.

Party Member

Aysha Salinte

A researcher with no clear memory of how long she's actually lived in Sengetsuki. Joins the party to get closer to the mystery everyone else is chasing.
