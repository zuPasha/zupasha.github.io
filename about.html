About

Three names, one project

zuPasha, TsuPasai, and VantaPsy describe the same person and the same world from three different distances.

Creator

zuPasha

A stylised contraction of Zuhayr Pasha. Zuhayr means bright, Pasha means general or leader. The lowercase "zu" is an intimate prefix; the capitalised "Pasha" carries the legacy name. This is the name behind the work.

Studio

TsuPasai

Built by reshaping how a Japanese speaker might pronounce zuPasha, then compressing it into something new. Roughly: moon soul leader, or guided psyche. For English speakers it sounds like "Super Psy," which is exactly the point.

World

VantaPsy

Vanta, to lack or want. Psy, soul. Together: to lack a soul, or to want one. The central question of every story, character, and system built under this name.

Background

Who's behind it

I'm a solo game developer, character artist, and animator based in South Africa, working under the name zuPasha. I hold a BA in Motion Design and Animation, completed across The Animation School and SAE Institute in Cape Town.

VantaPsy: Soul Snatcher is my flagship project, built in Ren'Py with a heavily extended custom engine. Everything else I make, the lore, the character art, the commission work, grows out of the same world and the same set of questions about identity, desire, and what it means to become a person.

Philosophy

The Six Steps of Humanity

Every major character in VantaPsy moves through some version of this structure. It is the philosophical backbone of the world.

  1. 01

    To Truly Lack

    Every journey begins with absence: love, family, identity, purpose, belonging, or hope. Lack is the condition that gives desire meaning.

  2. 02

    To Want with Compassion

    Desire gives direction to suffering. Compassion turns that desire into the possibility of real connection.

  3. 03

    To Seek Clarity

    People search for answers through knowledge, faith, obsession, or violence. Clarity means learning to see oneself and others honestly.

  4. 04

    To Act with Noble Intent

    Understanding alone changes nothing. Every character eventually acts, and every action carries unforeseen consequences.

  5. 05

    To Take Responsibility

    Growth begins with owning those consequences, the good and the harm both.

  6. 06

    To Echo with Reverence

    Every action leaves an imprint beyond the individual. A meaningful life is measured by the echoes it leaves in others.
