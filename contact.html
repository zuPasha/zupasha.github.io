Rights & Usage

Who owns what

The short version. The full terms are in the commission agreement you'll receive once a sketch is approved.

It's yours once it's paid for

Once payment is complete, the final artwork is yours outright, including reproduction, distribution, and commercial use.

I keep the right to show it off

Finished commissions get posted to the TsuPasai portfolio and social channels. That's part of how this work keeps going. If you'd rather your piece stayed private, just say so and I'll leave it out.

Credit is your call

Want a credit line when it's shown? I'll add one. Want to stay anonymous? That works too, just let me know which you'd prefer.

FAQ

Before you reach out

Q. Not sure what you want?

That's actually the most common starting point. The Discover step exists for exactly that. Bring whatever you have, even if it's just a feeling or a memory, and we'll work out the rest together.

Q. How long does a piece take?

Depends on the queue and the scope of the piece. Timelines are confirmed at the quote stage.

Q. What can't you take on?

Environment illustration, animation, large comics, highly realistic portraiture, and full branding packages aren't part of what I advertise, but I'm open to discussing them privately if it's a good fit. My focus stays on character art, narrative illustration, and design work built around meaning.
