VantaPsy Chronology

Echoes of The Anechoic

To lack a soul; to want a soul. That ache is where every story begins.

A dark rural-modern fantasy set within the wider VantaPsy Chronology, blending folklore, industrial fantasy, surrealism and cosmic horror into literary fiction exploring desire, the nature of the soul, and the ways love, suffering and inherited wounds shape what we become. It expands the world of VantaPsy: Soul Snatcher through stories and characters beyond the game, and begins the first arc of the Chronology.
